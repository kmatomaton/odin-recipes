Pancakes

Description

Simple fluffy pancake recipe, like in the breakfast restaurants

Prep Time: 5 min | Cook Time: 10 min | Yield: 6 pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cups flour
  • 1 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • butter, for cooking

double the ingredients for more pancakes

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients in a big bowl, until homogenous
  2. Melt a small amount of butter in a pan at medium high heat
  3. Pour 1/3 cup of mix into the pan and cook each side until golden
  4. Reserve cooked pancakes in a dish under foil. Repeat until the mix is done
  5. Serve pancakes with favorite toppings
