Sweet & Spicy Dumplings

Description

Dumplings in sweet & spicy maple sauce. Quick and easy weeknight meal.

Prep Time: 5 min Cook Time: 30-40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp puree ginger
  • 1/4 cup red curry paste
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 can coconut milk (398ml)
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 16-18 frozen pork and vegetable dumplings
  • Green onions and chili oil, to serve

Steps

  1. Set oven to 375F
  2. Combine all ingredients in a oven dish
  3. Cook in oven 30 to 40min
  5. Serve with white rice
