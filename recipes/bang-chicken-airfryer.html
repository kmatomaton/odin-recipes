Air Fryer Bang-Bang Chicken

Description

Easy no frills sweet & spicy chicken recipe. Perfect for quick weekday nights.

Prep Time: 5 min | Cooking Time: ~ 15 min

Ingredients

  • 3-4 chicken breasts
  • 1 sliced avocado
  • sliced green onions

Seasoning

  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper

Sauce

  • 1/3 cup mayo
  • 1/3 cup sweet chili sauce
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Steps

  1. Mix seasoning and sauce ingredients in separate bowls. Reserve a small amount of sauce
  2. Criss-cross cut the chicken breasts and rub in the seasoning mix
  3. Rub the sauce mix on top of the seasoned chicken breasts
  4. Air fry at 375F for 15 min, or until the chicken reaches 74C
  5. Serve with rice, sliced avocado and green onions. Drizzle with reserved sauce.
